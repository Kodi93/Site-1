As first spotted by BleepingComputer, ChatGPT has rolled out a new update to its GPT-5 instant model. This model update allows GPT-5 to quickly understand and support those who are not having a good time emotionally. "When GPT-5 detects you're having distress. The model is trained to more accurately detect and respond to potential signs of mental and emotional distress," OpenAI noted . OpenAI developed the model with help from mental health experts. GPT now automatically tries to push you towards real-world crisis resources when appropriate, while still using language that feels supportive and grounding. GPT-5 was disliked by GPT-4 users because it felt too practical when they were expecting emotional support. OpenAI says these changes will still keep GPT-5 supportive and grounded. "When GPT-5 Auto or a non-reasoning model is selected, we'll instead route these conversations to GPT-5 Instant to more quickly provide helpful and beneficial responses," OpenAI explained. "ChatGPT will continue to tell users which model is active when asked." This update to GPT-5 Instant is starting to roll out to ChatGPT users today.
