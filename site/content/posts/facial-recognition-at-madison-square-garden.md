Society in general has expectations of “public social spaces” and those expectations should be respected by the title holders if they wish to continue their commercial activities. The fact they tried to keep their behaviours against public social space expectations hidden clearly indicates they knew not just that what they were doing was socially unacceptable, but also that it’s legality was questionable thus draw down the ire not just of society but the authorities as well. Now I can not say if Ms Swift was being overly sensitive with respect to her private life on what is traditionally a very important day of her life, or if she had entered into contractual agreements with respect to the making the event partially public thus available in a controlled way[1] but she had expectations of some measure of privacy in what would otherwise have been seen differently as a public social space. As an accepted social rule, at a wedding what happens in the church is actually not considered public but private to a chosen group. However the photographs taken in the light of day outside the church or in the public park is in effect an announcement to the world and the start of a new day hence the expression “wedding breakfast”. It is a social custom to see this and wish the couple well for their lives to come, but it is also still considered private even though it’s a public announcement. Thus as an observer the expectation is you will walk on with private good wishes not get your mobile phone out and photograph every thing. The problem is technology makes the latter not just inexpensively possible but highly probable if there is even a hint of “celebrity” involved. So ask yourself how the brides expectations of long accepted social customs can be granted when there is so much commercial interest to do the opposite with what is now ubiquitous effectively minimal cost technology? It is this conflict of expectations that is causing society as we have understood it just a very short time ago to be obliterated, and in general society can not move that fast or even want to move in that direction.
