## How Combination Locks Work

A combination lock is a type of lock that uses a sequence of symbols, usually numbers or letters, to open. Unlike a key lock, a combination lock does not require a physical key. Instead, you must enter the correct sequence in the correct order. If any part of the sequence is wrong, the lock stays closed.

There are several types of combination locks. The most common is the single-dial lock, where you rotate a dial to specific numbers in a specific direction. There are also multi-dial locks, where each dial must be set to the correct character. Digital combination locks use a keypad where you type in a code. In all cases, the principle is the same: you need the right combination of characters in the right order.

In the context of this puzzle, the vault combination lock is a metaphor. The vault represents the final answer, and the combination is the specific sequence of letters and numbers that makes the answer complete. Without the correct combination, the vault stays locked and your answer is not valid.
