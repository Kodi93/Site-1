Meet Sarah, an artisanal baker who opens Sarah’s Sourdough. To improve her search engine optimization (SEO), she builds a beautiful website and shares authentic baking content. By writing blog posts, earning local backlinks and telling her story, Sarah practices ethical SEO to help search engines understand her value. Soon, when users search “fresh sourdough near me,” her shop ranks at the top. This is how search is meant to work – connecting real people with real solutions.
